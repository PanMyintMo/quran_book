# Privacy Policy for Hidayatul Quran

Effective date: April 23, 2026

Hidayatul Quran ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## 1. Information We Collect

- Account information: email address and basic profile details when you sign in.
- User content: profile image or files you choose to upload.
- App usage data: limited technical information needed to keep the app secure and working (for example, app version and error details).

We do not sell your personal data.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data to:

- create and manage your account,
- provide app features (such as synchronization and file/profile updates),
- keep the app secure, reliable, and functional,
- respond to support requests.

## 3. Permissions

Hidayatul Quran does not require persistent access to your photo or video library.

- Media/files are accessed only when you explicitly choose a file using the system picker.
- Notification permission is used only to send app notifications (where enabled).

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the app, including:

- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Realtime Database
- Firebase Storage
- Firebase App Check

These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.
If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required.

## 6. Children

Our app is not directed to children under the minimum digital age in applicable jurisdictions, unless local law allows with appropriate consent.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
